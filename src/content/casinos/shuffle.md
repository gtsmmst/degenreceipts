## What I liked
It works and it pays. Deposits and withdrawals are quick once you're verified. The sportsbook took my action without flinching — rare, as the Yeet, Thrill and Gamdom pages show. Lots of slots and Originals, lots of promotions, a lottery and airdrops around the token.

## The KYC episode
Small sums had been flying in and out on a low verification level for ages. Then, after a good run on sports, I requested a big withdrawal — $30K or $60K, I don't remember which — and got hit with level 4: proof of funds, proof of income. Fine, I have those. The problem was the bot. Whatever I sent came back "doesn't match". Support's answer: the robot checks documents, we can't do anything. For several days my money sat frozen while I photographed the same papers at different angles and in different light. Eventually one set went through. Same documents, different photo. Since then: no issues, withdrawals at proper sizes go through.

**Lesson:** if you plan to play big here, complete all four levels *before* the day you need a withdrawal.

## VIP
Honestly modest. Level-ups don't pay much. I'm told that at the higher tiers you get a host and can negotiate individual terms — I'm at $400K wagered and haven't got there, so I can't confirm it.

## Verdict
Good. If the KYC bot hadn't eaten three days of my life it would be "great". An old, stable room that pays and still lets me bet sports.

**Best for:** players who want a proven casino with a real sportsbook and are willing to verify fully up front.
