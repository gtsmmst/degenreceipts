## VIP and bonuses
They offered me a VIP transfer when I arrived, with a quest to complete: a big wagering target within 30 days. I didn't make it — only slots count toward the target, and I mostly bet on sports. Still, I wagered plenty there. The program is fine: lossback, a decent referral system, nothing huge yet. Bonuses are modest compared with Stake, which is fair for a casino this young.

## Sports: the problem
Yeet uses the same sportsbook provider as Stake and Shuffle, and its odds were often a point or two better. Then I had a short winning streak — nothing dramatic, maybe +$30–50K — and the limits started shrinking. First reduced, then cut to almost zero: on Sep 9, 2026 an esports bet at 4.20 was rejected with "max stake is $64.84".

I wrote to the founder, Keyboard Monkey. He was straight about it:

> "We don't really have anyone else betting your size multiple days out on matches. If you're interested in betting more the day of then we can accommodate, but sadly I don't know that we can accommodate high limits in these exact spots yet. Really unfortunate, as I want more esports action as well, but it's difficult for our book considering the esports volume isn't high yet."

Fair enough for a young book, but I bet esports days before matches, so that was the end for me. On Stake the VIP host fights for limits; Yeet doesn't have that weight yet.
