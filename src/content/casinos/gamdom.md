## What I liked
A polished, busy site with a huge slot library. The rewards program is good — quests, King of the Hill races, rakeback — and the VIP transfer terms were fair. Deposits instant, withdrawals confirmed without drama once verification was done.

## KYC
Be ready for this: I had levels 1 and 2 done, requested a withdrawal of a few thousand dollars, and got asked for level 3. Not a huge sum. The annoying part wasn't the request — it was the same document being rejected again and again, about ten rounds, before it was accepted. After that, everything worked.

## The sports tab
I'm a sports bettor. At Yeet and Thrill they cut my limits; at Gamdom they didn't bother with limits — the Sports tab stopped working for my account. Open it and you get an empty page and a toast: "FAILED. Your account has been banned from certain activities. If you need assistance, please contact our support department." No explanation, no warning. I hadn't won much there; I'd barely played. How these rooms decide who gets flagged, I still don't know. The result is the same: I left.

## Verdict
Nothing to complain about on the casino side. If you play slots and like reward programs, it's a solid place. If you bet sports, expect to be shown the door eventually.

**Best for:** slots players who enjoy quests and rakeback.
**Not for:** sports bettors.
