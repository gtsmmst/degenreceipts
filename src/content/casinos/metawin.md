## How I got here
I had accounts on Stake, Rollbit, Shuffle and SolCasino as far back as 2023, but gambling didn't grab me. MetaWin is where I actually dipped my toes in, so it still feels like home turf. Then MetaWin launched with its NFT collection and daily raffles: an expensive NFT, $5–10K, drawn every day, and you just buy a ticket or a few. Early on there weren't many players, and I won a few times — $5K, $3K, a couple of cheaper NFTs. That hooked me. The sums were small, but I spent a lot of time there.

## Deposits and withdrawals
Wallet-based: MetaMask or Rabby, deposit ETH, USDT or USDC, play, withdraw. Withdrawals land instantly; above a few thousand you confirm it once more, that's all. My sums here were small, under $14K per withdrawal, so I can't speak for six figures. Every transaction in the account history links to Etherscan.

## KYC
None. As far as I've seen, MetaWin doesn't ask at all.

## Sports
Almost none: football, basketball, tennis, and that's it. For a long time sports bets didn't even count toward wagering; now they do, partly, above certain odds. Players keep asking for more sports. Once I started betting on sports seriously, this is why I left.

## VIP and bonuses
Looked fine when I started; now I'd call it average, 5 out of 10. Daily and weekly bonuses, rank-up rewards, constant raffles. I never climbed high here, so bigger levels may be better.

## Games
Slots, Originals, raffles, the daily "Monthly Million" style draws. Enough to do, but the site is a bit clunky and lags at times.

## The hack
In November 2024 MetaWin lost about $4M from hot wallets on Ethereum and Solana. Withdrawals were paused, then restored; the CEO said he covered the losses, partly from his own pocket. I had no money stuck, and I've seen no repeat since.

## Verdict
A decent casino that didn't fit me. If you're a slots and raffles player who likes playing straight from a wallet, it's fine. If you bet on sports, look elsewhere.

**Best for:** slots and raffle players who want no-KYC, wallet-based play.
